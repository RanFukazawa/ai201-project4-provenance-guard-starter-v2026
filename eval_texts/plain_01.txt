The process of making own choices and decisions is an essential human intelligence to cogitate problems logically and precisely. The process is also known as “agency” – the ability to make own decisions and to influence society. Agency is developed through learning and experiencing from external factors. Childhood is a sensitive time that children can absorb every new knowledge and information without almost no hesitation. Hence, children accept anything, including the concept of morally and ethically right or wrong. One way of learning principles is from literature. Literature contains abundant thoughts and conceptions of the predecessors that has the power to impact on changing readers’ minds.
