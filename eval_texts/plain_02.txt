To fulfil my loneliness, I was obsessed with collecting the stuffed animals when I was small. Many environment changes had made me challenging to make new friends, and it was weary for me to adjust to the new circumstance. Filling one side of the bed with stuffed animals was the visual form to recognise that I was not solitary. Even though they are objects that cannot speak or move, their physical existence helped me feel a sense of safety and tranquility. It minimized my aloneness from being physically and mentally isolated from the community.
