Throughout the readings, I realized that “the U.S. benevolent assimilation” often appeared, which referred to the U.S. imperialism in the Philippines as well as the U.S. response towards the Filipino immigrants in the U.S. It was interesting to see how the U.S. strived for obscuring its evident subjugation in the Philippines by using positive words. The Filipinos were another target of the U.S. exploitation of immigrant labor forces along with other Asian immigrants who had migrated before their arrival, and they were not the exception of the anti-Asian sentiment.
