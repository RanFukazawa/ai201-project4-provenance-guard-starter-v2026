The Pantheon, built in Rome in the second century AD,  is considered to have been designed as a temple to honor all Roman gods, which was later used as a Christian church. It is made of brick, concrete and white marble, consisting of three sections: a portico with free-standing columns topped by a triangular pediment, a colossal domed rotunda, and a massive rectangular pronaos connecting the portico and the rotunda. In front of the Pantheon, there is the Fontana del Pantheon, or Fountain of the Pantheon. The Founders Hall in Soka University of America, a third story domed building, was opened in 2001 on top of the hill, surrounded by canyons and is located at Aliso Viejo, California. Copper, concrete and travertine stone are used and it consists of a portico with free-standing columns with a triangular pediment and a rectangular area with a copper domed ceiling. Peace Lake with two fountains opens out before the Founders Hall.
