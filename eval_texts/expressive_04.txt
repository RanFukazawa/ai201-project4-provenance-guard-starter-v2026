AUGUST: 	Neither! Winter was my pet chinchilla.

RIVER: 	Wait, what?! Chinchilla? Not human? Are you saying you transferred a chinchilla’s memories to my brain?

AUGUST: 	(annoyed) Well, yes. Why not?

RIVER: 	Is that why I don’t remember anything?

AUGUST: 	Yeah. (beat). I don’t need YOUR MEMORY. I don’t need you as a person. I just needed a perfect body to transfer Winter’s memory.

RIVER: 	………… Are you human? (beat) You’re evil (or crazy)!!

AUGUST: 	So, what? I don’t care how you feel. What I care about is my life with Winter. He was with me for 15 years. We slept together, played together, and ate together. But he died of old age. I can’t live without him anymore. I NEED him.
