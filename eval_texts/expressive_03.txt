Wind
The breeze blew gently,
Gently as caressing my cheeks,
But felt strangled around my neck,
That I felt a little discomfort.

It passed over me,
Giving me a glance,
But a hollow glance,
Like not being aware of my existence.

Brutally sweeping me away,
Ruthlessly drawing an imperceptible boundary,
But wholeheartedly embracing me,
That I cannot escape.

I felt ovetwhelmed,
I felt intimidated,
I felt suffocated,
I felt left out.

Where should I be?

Although it is caring,
I need to confess,
It is squalid and sinister,
That I cannot find myself.

When I looked up above, It was a bright blue, Vivid and blazing,
It was breathtakingly beautiful.

Like the shining sky and unpleasant breeze,
I realized how disharmonized the day had been, But this was the day I had had,
The similar day I might have for the next day.

I started walking again.
