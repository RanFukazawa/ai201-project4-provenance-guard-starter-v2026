The pyramid of Djoser is the oldest pyramid built in the period of the Third-Dynasty of pharaoh Djoser located at Saqqara necropolis, southwest of Cairo in Egypt. It is the first monument made of stone, beginning as a mastaba tomb – a rectangular structure with sloping walls and a flat roof – and evolving upward into 60 meters pyramid with six layers enclosed by stone walls. There is only one entrance in the southeast corner connected to a narrow colonnade.
