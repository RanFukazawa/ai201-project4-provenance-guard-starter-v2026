My English Journey
Every morning I open my book and read new English words, and my heart beats fast like a drum.
Sometimes I do not understand, and I feel small like a bird who cannot find its tree.
But my teacher smiles and says, “Try again, my friend, every mistake is a step to learning.”
So I practice every day, speaking slowly, writing little sentences, and dreaming in two languages at the same time.
One day my English will fly high, and I will tell my story to the whole wide world.
