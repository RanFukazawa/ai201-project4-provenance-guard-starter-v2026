Reapplying for undergraduate programs was the most challenging time I had to persevere, especially in coping with emotional perseverance. 
I was mentally defeated by being rejected by all colleges since I have been interested in Computer Science and thought getting into a CS major was the best way. 
It was the first significant setback I had in my life that resulted in completely losing self-confidence and fearing failure. 
Although earning a bachelor’s degree is not everything, a bachelor’s or a higher degree can open up more opportunities and is required in many professional jobs. 
Hence, I could not give up on pursuing my interest and decided to reapply to get my life back on track.
